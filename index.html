01 About Me

Who I am

I'm a data analyst who enjoys working with messy data. I like taking raw, unstructured datasets — the kind with missing values, duplicate rows, inconsistent dates, and wrong entries — and turning them into something clean, organized, and reliable. Only after that do I build dashboards, because a good dashboard is only as strong as the data behind it.

What I do

  • Clean messy and inconsistent datasets
  • Handle null values and incorrect inputs
  • Standardize date formats and data types
  • Remove duplicates and validate data
  • Explore data to find patterns and outliers
  • Build interactive dashboards in Power BI and Excel
